EDITORIAL POLICY

Make the evidence and the correction route visible

Each article should show who published it, the evidence used, the review scope and how to report a correction.

1. Topics and useful decisions

We cover buying choices, practical digital tasks and sports records that need explanation. A guide should help a reader make a decision, compare a cost or understand a result. Earlier settings and security guides remain an archive; a topic label is not a measured traffic or popularity claim.

2. Primary sources and their dates

We prioritize the responsible company’s documentation, official competition records and public-agency guidance. Source sections link the material actually referenced and preserve its check date. Reading documentation is not described as hands-on device experience. Unverified personal reports are not promoted to universal facts.

3. Facts, examples and tests

Official menu instructions, KYD’s hypothetical inputs and actual tests are separated. When a test is described, its inputs, expected behavior, environment and limits should be stated. Instructions explain symptoms, comparisons, safe next actions and stop conditions. Backup and consequences come before deletion, reset or permission changes. We do not replace official support or individual professional advice.

Buying and price information

  • State the model, country, capacity, currency and check date.
  • Distinguish list prices, actual contractual conditions and hypothetical quotes.
  • Keep device price separate from service, fees and conditional benefits.
  • Do not call an untested product a hands-on review or invent a performance ranking.
  • Publish calculation assumptions and correct confirmed errors.
  • Do not fabricate search volumes, visitors or personal product photographs.

Sports records and photographs

Use the official organizer and result system. Separate confirmed scores from schedules, selected matches from all-event totals, and competition dates from publication dates. Record the check time and timezone. Do not invent eyewitness observations or athlete quotations. Photographs require an identified source and reuse terms; captions distinguish archive venues or older products from the event or model discussed. Search-image availability is not permission.

4. Publishing name, translation and review dates

KYD Editorial is a site publishing name, not an assertion of multiple expert reviewers. Original publication dates remain visible. The English edition was adapted on October 5, 2026; substantial English changes are recorded as editorial modifications, while original source-check dates remain separate. We do not make information appear freshly investigated merely by translating it.

5. Corrections

Compare the report, article and official original. Prioritize errors affecting safety or essential instructions and record material corrections. Minor language edits may be made without a separate notice. The updates page records substantive changes.

6. Tools and verification

Tools support an explained task without requiring passwords, verification codes or file uploads. Calculation and search inputs are processed locally where stated. Results are not safety or recovery guarantees. Utility and result pages do not load advertising code. Published test scope is evidence of behavior under stated inputs, not malware detection or account inspection.

7. Advertising independence

Advertising is separate from editorial conclusions and navigation. Ads must not masquerade as menus, downloads or article links. Ad code is not proof of AdSense approval. Where applicable, advertising consent must be configured under the provider’s regional requirements before serving ads.

8. Automation-assisted work

Generative and automation tools assist drafts, translations and code. Sources, links, caution language and context are reviewed before publication. We do not invent experiences, statistics or credentials or present an automated output as independent professional verification.

9. Reporting

Use the public GitHub issue route. Do not post private files, account details, passwords, codes or payment information.
