iPhone 18 Pro vs Pro Max: is the extra KRW 200,000 worth it?

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-09-26 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

Start with the size you carry and the storage you need, not the most expensive model. In the South Korean price snapshot below, Pro 512GB costs KRW 100,000 more than Pro Max 256GB: the trade-off is storage versus a larger screen.

Market and date matter.

The product information was checked on Apple’s South Korean pages on September 26, 2026. Prices are Korean-won list prices, not US or worldwide offers, and exclude discounts, trade-in, service plans and optional coverage. Recheck before purchasing. This is not a hands-on review or a battery measurement.

Which “iPhone 18” model?

This comparison covers iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. The source check did not find a standard iPhone 18 in Apple’s model list on that date. Do not apply Pro specifications to an unverified standard model. This article does not predict future launch dates.

Three choices within a budget

  • Larger screen first: at a roughly KRW 2.2 million device budget, compare Max 256GB. Add accessories and coverage before deciding that the budget is sufficient.
  • More local video storage, less size: compare Pro 512GB with Max 256GB. The extra KRW 100,000 buys a different capacity trade-off, not the Max screen. Calculate needs in the storage guide.
  • No clear capacity or size problem: a higher product tier does not create a reason to replace a phone. Write the current inconvenience in one sentence first.

Put the difference in context without hiding the total

KRW 200,000 divided by 24 months is approximately KRW 8,333; divided by 36 it is approximately KRW 5,556. This is simple arithmetic, not an installment bill. The total extra purchase cost remains KRW 200,000.

A short in-store comparison

  1. Hold each model as you normally would and check thumb reach. Security fixtures can change the perceived weight.
  2. Read the same text and map at the same text-size setting.
  3. Check pocket or bag dimensions with room for a case, following staff instructions.
  4. Compare camera use for the subjects and distances that matter to you. A short display-unit test does not establish night or long-session performance.
  5. Write the exact model, capacity, full device price and optional coverage separately.

Reasons to investigate before buying

If the battery is the only problem, compare official inspection and repair quotes with replacement cost. If one app or AI feature motivates the purchase, verify current region, language, subscription and app requirements; promised future support is not a feature available today. Check remaining installments, contract conditions and resale assumptions before choosing a purchase route.

The plan can outweigh the model difference

A KRW 10,000 monthly service difference becomes KRW 240,000 over 24 months, larger than this model price gap. Use the phone-cost comparator and worked cost example. Outside South Korea, enter your actual local quotes in one currency rather than converting this old snapshot into a claimed local offer.

Write the decision rule first

Fix the budget ceiling and required storage, then test handling and screen needs. If a bigger screen exceeds the budget, do not blindly reduce necessary storage: keeping the current phone is another option. Delay when you cannot explain the problem being solved or cannot verify a required feature. These are KYD decision prompts, not product-performance test results.
