Asian Games October 3: Korea football, Lee Woo-seok and Song Da-bin take gold

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-10-05 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

South Korea beat Japan 1–0 in the men’s football final with EOM Jisung’s second-half goal. Lee Woo-seok and Song Da-bin also won gold, while Oh Ye-jin and Hongten took silver. These matches were played on October 3; the source article was published on October 5.

Men’s football: EOM Jisung scores at 61′

The gold-medal match began at 19:30 at Toyota Stadium. The official record shows EOM Jisung, 61′, with a 0–0 first half and 1–0 second half. Duration is listed as 90:00. The 1–0 result is the match goal score, not a penalty shoot-out. The China–Uzbekistan bronze match was a separate event.

The real Toyota Stadium photograph shows a J.League match in 2010, not this goal, these spectators or the 2026 final.

Recurve archery: gold and shoot-off silver

Lee Woo-seok won the 14:50 men’s final against Japan’s Junya Nakanishi, 6–2 in set points. His points by set were 2, 1, 1, 2: wins in the first and fourth, draws in the middle. Six is not the total arrow score.

In the 10:50 women’s final, Oh Ye-jin faced India’s MOHOD Kumkum Anil. Regular-set points were 2, 1, 0, 2, 0 versus 0, 1, 2, 0, 2, totaling 5–5. Oh’s shoot-off arrow scored 8; the opponent’s X scored ten. The deciding set point gave the opponent a final 6, leaving Oh with silver at 5–6.

Taekwondo: Song Da-bin wins women’s +67kg

Song defeated China’s MU Wenzhe 2–1 in the gold-medal match beginning at 17:10 at Toyohashi Gymnasium. The record marks Song Gold, MU Silver and the match Official. The result is not a football-like single-point margin, and it does not provide a count of successful techniques or a success rate.

Breaking: Hongten takes silver

Hongten (KIM Hongyul) lost the B-Boys final 0–3 to Japan’s Shigekix (NAKARAI Shigeyuki) at Aichi Sky Expo Hall F, beginning at 17:06. Losing this final still means a Silver medal, not leaving without one. The breaking result should not be interpreted as LoL game wins or recurve set points.
