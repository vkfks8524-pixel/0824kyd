Asian Games October 4 equestrian finals: medals, penalties and Korean results

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-10-05 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

The October 4 final-day schedule includes individual jumping A and B at Tokyo’s Equestrian Park. Qatar’s Faleh Alajami won A; Saudi Arabia’s Ramzy Alduhami won B. Read final ranks, round penalties and jump-off times as separate fields.

Individual jumping A and B medals

The official A title specifies 1.40–1.50m; B specifies up to 1.55m. They are different events, not one combined ranking. Listed times below are scheduled starts, not finish times.

South Korean final ranks

The names retain the official record’s Latin spelling. LEE Hyeongyu competed in A with EOLE DU GUERPRE and is listed at final Rank 10 with Result 12. His second-round Total Penalties was 4 and Time was 78.21 seconds. JUNG Chul Hee competed in B with ZENSATIONAL, Rank 14 and Result 32, with second-round penalties 9 and time 82.23 seconds. Neither has a medal marker in these final tables.

Result and a single round’s Total Penalties are different columns. A’s 12 and 4 cannot both be described as the same round’s penalties, and B’s 32 is not simply its final-round 9. The table does not infer individual obstacle errors or horse condition.

Why 42.59 seconds ranked above 39.73

The B second-round summary lists all three leading athletes with Result 0. Its separate Jump-Off record resolves the order:

Alduhami won with zero penalties despite not having the fastest time. Alrajhi and Mohammed each had eight penalties, so their 39.73 and 43.26-second times distinguish them. Read penalties and time together. The second-round times of 76.96, 76.42 and 73.76 seconds are different runs from the jump-off’s 42.59, 39.73 and 43.26. A has a separate jump-off; do not apply B’s figures to it.

Keep rounds and status markers distinct

The records separate the October 3 first round from the October 4 second round and jump-off. A first-round ranking is not a final medal result. The table legend identifies RT, WD and EL as Retired, Withdrawn and Eliminated; do not turn them into zero penalties or normal completed ranks.

Closing ceremony and the next Games

OCA’s announcement records the October 4 evening closing ceremony at Nagoya’s Mizuho Park Athletics Stadium and the flag handover to Qatar for the 2030 Doha Asian Games. The archive image shows Tokyo JRA Equestrian Park’s gate on August 4, 2017, not a 2026 ride or ceremony.
