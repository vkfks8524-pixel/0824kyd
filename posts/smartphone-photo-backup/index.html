iPhone and Android photo backups: six checks before deleting originals

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-07-12 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

“Backup switched on” and “I can reopen the original” are different pieces of evidence. Before deleting photos or changing phones, check the account, upload state, actual files, videos, extra folders and independent copies.

iCloud sync is not two independent photo copies

Apple’s iCloud Photos guidance explains that edits and deletions synchronize across devices using the library. Items in iCloud Photos are not duplicated again in the iCloud device backup. Seeing a photo on two phones signed into the same account therefore does not establish two independent backups.

After Android’s Free up space operation, items may not be available in the device gallery or offline. Do not assume an iPhone uses the same menu or workflow.

Pair six checks with evidence

Google distinguishes the overall backup state, individual item status and device-folder settings. The following is KYD’s checklist for noticing those differences, not a certification test.

Matching counts do not finish the check

Hypothetical example: a camera folder contains 980 photos and 20 videos, while a messenger folder contains 70 more images. A web library showing 1,000 items does not prove those 70 images were included. A count difference does not establish loss either: compare duplicates, accounts and display scope. These numbers are illustrative, not the owner’s photo count.

The next action is to find important recent and older originals in each folder and open them elsewhere—not delete them. Record a thumbnail and an opened original as different evidence.

Record location and reopening results privately

Download cloud originals or copy device files outside the synchronized library, then open the photo and video files. Review export formats if Live Photos, edits or originals matter. Do not publish account addresses, private images or the backup location to prove that you used this guide.
