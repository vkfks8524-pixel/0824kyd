Asian Games October 2: Korea wins LoL and men’s archery gold

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-10-02 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

South Korea won the League of Legends final 3–0 against Chinese Taipei. Kim Je-deok, Kim Woo-jin and Lee Woo-seok took men’s recurve-team gold. NOH Jinyoung’s fourth-minute goal gave the women’s football team a 1–0 bronze-match win over China.

LoL: a final score of 3–0

The final at Aichi Sky Expo Hall D is marked Finals and Official, with South Korea Gold and Chinese Taipei Silver. The previous day’s 2–0 semifinal against Saudi Arabia is a separate match. A 3–0 final represents three games won, not kills or an in-game gold advantage. Final scores alone do not support player-by-player tactical or draft analysis.

Men’s recurve: draw, win, win

Kim Je-deok, Kim Woo-jin and Lee Woo-seok defeated India 5–1 in set points. Arrow scores were 56–56, 57–50 and 56–54. The drawn opening set gave each side one point; the next two wins gave South Korea two each.

Women’s recurve: three draws and one decisive set

Kang Chae-young, Lee Yun-ji and Oh Ye-jin took silver after a 3–5 loss to India. Arrow scores were 54–54, 56–56, 51–55 and 54–54. South Korea’s set points were 1, 1, 0, 1, while India’s were 1, 1, 2, 1. Three of four sets were drawn; the third set supplied India’s decisive two points.

Mixed recurve bronze

Kang Chae-young and Kim Je-deok beat Japan 5–1. Arrow scores were 39–35, 37–37 and 37–35, producing cumulative set points of 2–0, 3–1 and 5–1. Unlike the men’s team final, the mixed match’s second set was drawn. Mixed pairs shoot four arrows per set for a maximum of 40, while three-person teams shoot six for a maximum of 60; comparing 39 and 56 as the same scale would be misleading.

Women’s football: NOH Jinyoung at 4′

South Korea beat China 1–0 at Shizuoka Stadium Ecopa. The official record names NOH Jinyoung as the scorer at 4′. The stage is 3rd Place Match and the medal is Bronze, not the women’s gold-medal final or a men’s football result.
