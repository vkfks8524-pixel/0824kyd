2026 Asian Games final medal standings: China first, Japan second, Korea third

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-10-05 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

After the October 4 closing ceremony, the official all-disciplines table places China first with 169 gold, Japan second with 83 and South Korea third with 39. South Korea’s 39 gold, 43 silver and 68 bronze total 150 medal events.

South Korea: 39 gold, 150 overall

The calculation is 39 + 43 + 68 = 150. Gold counts winning medal events; total includes all three medal types. Describing 39 as all medals, or 150 as wins, changes the meaning.

The percentages divide each count by 150 and round to one decimal place. They are KYD calculations, not extra points used for official rankings. A team-event medal counts once in the delegation’s event table even if several athletes receive medals. The total 150 is therefore not a count of 150 individual medal recipients.

More total medals can still mean a lower rank

  • India and Uzbekistan both have 21 gold. India’s 27 silver precedes Uzbekistan’s 25, placing them fourth and fifth.
  • Hong Kong and Bahrain each have 11 gold; their 19 and 5 silver separate ninth and tenth.
  • Kazakhstan has 84 total versus Thailand’s 52, but Thailand has 18 gold versus Kazakhstan’s 12 and ranks seventh rather than eighth.

Japan exceeds South Korea by 44 gold (83−39) and 119 total (269−150); South Korea exceeds India by 18 gold (39−21) and 65 total (150−85). Those differences alone do not establish athlete quality, delegation size or investment efficiency. The article does not analyze those factors.

Closing date and next host

OCA reports the October 4 evening closing ceremony at Mizuho Park Athletics Stadium, Nagoya, and the flag handover to Qatar for the 2030 Doha Games. Delegation totals do not reveal an individual match’s scorer or winning sequence. For those details, read the October 3 selected finals and October 4 equestrian results.
