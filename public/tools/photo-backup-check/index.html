    How to read the result

    Self-check 2/6 means you marked two of six items. It does not mean a 33% backup success rate or that only two photos were saved. Remaining checks follow account → upload → originals → video → folders → independent copy. Resolve uncertainty before deletion or reset.

    Hypothetical example: If you checked only the account and upload state, originals, video, additional folders and an independent copy remain. Changing a selection updates an existing result immediately. Reset clears checkboxes; it does not erase a device or photos.
