Suggested review order

    Why calculate a target before cleanup?

    Deleting photos or documents solely because of a storage warning can lose valuable data. A 3GB gap differs from a 30GB gap. Review replaceable downloads and trash first; larger gaps may require independent photo copies and unused-app review. A calculated gap does not prove any file is disposable.

    The calculator multiplies total capacity by the selected percentage, then subtracts current free space. Manufacturers, systems, updates and workloads vary; the result is a planning number, not a performance guarantee.

    Storage and memory are different

    Storage holds photos, videos, apps and documents. Memory (RAM) is working space for running apps and the system. Storage cleanup alone cannot fix every app termination or multitasking problem.

    Nearly full storage can prevent app installation, updates, captures or file saving. Android documentation recommends inspecting storage categories and reviewing backed-up or unneeded items before freeing space.

    A cautious cleanup order

    1. Trash and downloads: Review replaceable installers, old PDFs and trash contents before permanently removing anything.
    2. Offline content: Check whether music or video downloads can be obtained again through your account.
    3. Unused apps: Inspect internal data and login access first. Removing cache differs from removing app data.
    4. Messenger media: Inspect large videos and duplicates by size; keep important conversation material separately.
    5. Photos and videos: Do not rely solely on a completed-backup badge. Open important originals on another device or the web and verify deletion behavior.

    Separate cloud capacity from device capacity

    Full Google Drive or Google Photos cloud storage is different from full phone storage. Depending on synchronization, device deletion can also remove cloud items; cloud-only cleanup may not immediately free the phone. Inspect sync, copies and trash retention before deletion.

    Calculation limits

    This does not read device files, system-reserved space, SD cards or cloud state. Advertised and system-displayed capacity can differ. Meeting the selected target does not rule out update errors, damaged apps or other causes.
