## Processing and retention

Source photos are resized/cropped to 3:2 and encoded as WebP by scripts/prepare-editorial-photos.cjs. No generative editing, screen replacement, model alterations, or retouching. The portrait storage photo is cropped around the handset; the budget photo retains its composition; the choice photo is center-cropped. The source-page camera information identifies the choice photo as SONY ILCE-7SM3 and the budget photo as Canon EOS 5D Mark IV. The choice page labels iPhone 17 Pro, but depicts different generations; KYD deliberately does not identify every model or present it as iPhone 18.
