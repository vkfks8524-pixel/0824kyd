Amazon says delivered, but no package? What to check and when to contact support

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-10-06 · Last editorial review 2026-10-06

Open the actual order in Amazon, identify the package marked delivered, and compare its delivery details with the places you have checked. A missing parcel needs a clear record of what happened. That record is more useful than repeatedly refreshing the same tracking message.

Scope and evidence

The timing and support routes below reference Amazon.com’s US help page, checked October 6, 2026. Other Amazon stores, carriers and order types can have different rules. Use the instructions attached to your own order. KYD is independent of Amazon and does not receive your order details.

First, identify which problem you actually have

Start from the Amazon app or the storefront where you placed the order. Open Your Orders, select the item and read that package’s tracking details. Write down the displayed status before choosing a help route. “My order is missing” can describe several different situations.

Still in transit or running lateThe current delivery estimate and latest scanThat a delivered-package procedure applies

This table organizes your observations; it does not determine responsibility. If you have a box with damaged packaging or missing contents, preserve the box and take your own factual photographs before discarding anything. Tell support which situation occurred rather than selecting a convenient label that does not fit.

Follow the official missing-package checks

Amazon.com’s missing-delivered-package help page asks customers to verify the order’s shipping address, look for delivery notices or a photo, check the requested delivery location, and ask household members or neighbors whether they accepted it. The carrier may also have location information.

The page advises allowing 48 hours because a package can be scanned as delivered before it arrives. If it remains missing, the page directs customers to the seller when a third party both sold and shipped the order. For an Amazon-shipped order, it says to contact Customer Service within 30 days of expected delivery. These are the checked US-page instructions, not a universal refund entitlement or a reason to ignore a different deadline shown on your order.

Read the order-specific help before relying on a timer. If the delivery information appears wrong, preserve it when you notice it and use the available help route to clarify what you should do. A waiting step should not erase the evidence you will need later.

Compare the photo with the location, not with a suspicion

When a delivery image is available, look for stable details: the door color, number placement, floor surface or recognizable parcel-room shelf. Record a concrete discrepancy such as “the image shows a red door; this building’s entrance is blue.” A factual comparison is easier to investigate than “the driver stole it,” which you may have no evidence to support.

Check only places you can access safely and with permission. A photo resembling another home is not permission to enter its property. For an apartment building, asking the reception desk whether a parcel was logged can be more useful than wandering between floors.

Keep the full-resolution evidence for the official support conversation. If you ask friends or a public forum for advice, remove names, addresses, order identifiers and readable label codes from the copy you share. A public discussion does not need the same details as the order’s authenticated support channel.

Build a short timeline before contacting anyone

Use one note with three columns: time, observation and action. Keep what the screen says separate from what you personally checked. This avoids turning a tracking event into a claim that you witnessed the delivery.

The times are invented. Replace them with your own observations and local time zone. Do not copy the example as if those checks happened. If you did not ask a neighbor, write “not yet checked” instead of filling in a complete-looking list.

A message that makes the next step clear

In the support conversation opened from your order, explain the discrepancy, the checks completed and the outcome you need clarified. Here is an original KYD template. It is a draft to adapt, not a message that this website sends for you.

I am contacting you about the package linked to this order. Tracking shows delivered at [date and local time], but I have not located it. I checked [places actually checked] and asked [people actually asked]. The delivery photo [matches / differs from / is not available for] the expected location; the specific detail is [observation]. I followed the waiting or follow-up instruction shown for my order: [instruction and time]. Please confirm the appropriate next step and any deadline I need to meet.

Keep the reply, case reference and next agreed action together. If support asks you to return later, record the exact date and what you are meant to check then. Avoid starting several separate conversations with contradictory descriptions; a consistent record makes it easier to explain the unresolved point.

Use the order’s support route, not a search-result phone number

Amazon’s guidance on impersonation scams recommends checking messages through its app or website and contacting support there. It warns against phone numbers from unsolicited messages or search results. It also says support will not require a gift-card payment, remote access or software installation to connect with customer service.

A delivery problem creates an easy moment to trust a message that arrives at the right time. Compare any new request with the actual order you opened yourself. If a text asks you to pay a small redelivery charge, do not infer that it is authentic just because you are waiting for a parcel. Find the request independently in the relevant official account or carrier service.

Our local URL structure checker can show which hostname a link contains. It cannot certify that a website, caller or payment request is trustworthy. You do not need to paste a private tracking URL into it to use the order’s normal help route.

Before placing the next time-sensitive order

Review the delivery destination before checkout, particularly after moving or sending a gift. Write instructions that a delivery person can actually follow, and avoid publishing access codes in public conversations. For shared buildings, establish who checks the parcel area and how recipients are notified.

If a pickup location is offered for the particular order, compare its opening hours, collection requirements and your availability before selecting it. A location you cannot reach before its collection deadline is not a practical backup. For something needed on a fixed date, decide what you will do if the parcel arrives late before committing to the plan.

Common questions

Does “delivered” mean a refund is impossible?

The status alone does not establish the final outcome. Use the order’s help route and provide an accurate account. This guide cannot promise a refund, replacement or claim decision.

Do the US timing rules apply to Amazon UK, Germany or Japan?

Do not assume that they do. Open help in the storefront where you bought the item and follow its order-specific instructions.

What if the parcel turns up after I have contacted support?

Update the existing conversation and ask how any pending replacement or refund should be handled. Keep the record consistent with what actually happened.
