Asian Games September 29 results: An Se-young gold, Park Hye-jeong silver

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-09-29 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

An Se-young beat Akane Yamaguchi 2–0 in the women’s badminton singles final. Lee So-hee and Baek Ha-na recovered from a lost opening game to win the doubles final 2–1. Park Hye-jeong took weightlifting silver with a 300kg total.

An Se-young: 21–17, 21–9

The singles final at Ichinomiya Municipal Gymnasium lasted 52 minutes in the official record. The first-game margin was four points and the second was twelve. The combined points were 42–26, but that is only an explanatory calculation: winning two games determined the match, not a combined-point comparison. Read the 2–0 match result together with the individual game scores.

Lee So-hee and Baek Ha-na turn the final around

The doubles final lasted one hour and 51 minutes. South Korea lost the opening game 26–28 to China’s Liu Shengshu and Tan Ning, then won the next two 21–18, 21–18. The official table lists the Chinese pair first and therefore displays 28–26, 18–21, 18–21. The summary reverses the presentation to put South Korea first; these are not conflicting results.

The singles and doubles titles are the two badminton wins covered here. They are not a full tournament tally including team events or other dates.

Park Hye-jeong: 133kg + 167kg = 300kg

In the women’s +86kg final at Nagoya City Trade and Industry Center, Park’s best snatch was 133kg and clean and jerk was 167kg. China’s Li Wenwen won with 140kg and 170kg, totaling 310kg. The ten-kilogram gap comprises seven in the snatch and three in the clean and jerk. Park did not lift 300kg in a single attempt: TOTAL combines the two categories.
