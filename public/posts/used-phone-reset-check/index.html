Before selling your phone: verify the new device, accounts and eSIM

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-07-14 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

Prepare the new phone before erasing the old one. Photos appearing on the new device do not prove that authenticator apps, payment services and the phone line are ready for handover.

List what must keep working

Include photos, contacts, messenger history, authenticator tools, financial apps and transport or payment services. Transfer methods can differ. “Full backup complete” does not mean that every app has completed its migration.

Illustrative situation: photos appear on the new phone, but an important login still needs a code from the old device. Pause and inspect that service’s official transfer or recovery process before searching for the erase button. This is a hypothetical case, not a reproduced incident or a tested app review.

Give every stage a stop condition

StageVerify yourselfStop when
1. CopiesOpen important photos and files on the new device or separate copyThe only originals remain on the old phone
2. Account accessImportant app logins and recovery methods workAccess depends on an unavailable old-device code
3. Phone lineCalls, texts and SIM or eSIM migration on the new deviceYou cannot receive required calls or verification texts
4. Manufacturer procedureModel-specific account and erase instructionsManagement, loss protection or password issues remain
5. HandoverSetup screen, physical cards and external storageOwner authentication is still required or private files remain

Line transfer and eSIM reissue depend on the carrier and device. Apple distinguishes deleting an eSIM, obtaining a new eSIM and cancelling a plan. Contact the carrier for the latter two as applicable. Verify the new line first; this article does not cancel a subscription or transfer its ownership.

Follow the correct iPhone or Android procedure

iPhone: Apple’s sale or trade-in guidance begins with backup and transfer, then removal of personal information. Review Apple Watch unpairing if relevant. Deleting photos or contacts one by one while signed into iCloud can affect synchronized data. Sign-out options vary by iOS version; follow the current document for your version and its official erase procedure, rather than treating this short summary as every screen.

Android: Google’s reset guidance explains that device data is erased and account-security information may be needed for restoration. Verify access from another device and inspect the official waiting guidance if the Google Account password was recently changed. Manufacturer-specific menus and account-removal steps take priority for your model.

This is for an owner preparing their own device. It does not bypass another owner’s lock or a school or employer’s management controls. Do not solve a lock problem by giving the buyer your account password.

Document the handover without publishing identifiers

Record the condition and setup screen where useful. Keep full serial numbers, IMEI, phone numbers, account addresses and unlock codes out of public sale photographs or reports. Follow the transaction service’s process for any required private information.

New device: important files / logins / calls and texts
Old device: official procedure / setup screen
Separate checks: physical SIM / external memory / connected devices
Remaining issue: __________
Resolved before handover: __________

A setup screen does not automatically prove that every account lock or management policy has been removed. If the buyer’s setup needs previous-owner authentication, resolve it through the manufacturer’s normal process before completing handover.

When this sequence is not enough

  • For a lost device, use the manufacturer’s lost-device guidance; the objective differs from resale.
  • For a broken screen, do not assume backup or sign-out is complete. Seek official support.
  • For a work or school device, obtain the required management approval first.
  • If photo copies are uncertain, return to the six photo checks and preserve originals.
