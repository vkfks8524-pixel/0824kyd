Asian Games September 30–October 1: Kim Jong-ho gold, Korea reaches LoL final

By KYD Editorial · First published 2026-10-01 · Last editorial review 2026-10-05

English edition adapted on 2026-10-05. The original source-check date is retained below; this is not a fresh product or match investigation.

Kim Jong-ho won the men’s compound individual final 149–145 on October 1. Kang Yeon-seo won the women’s individual bronze match, while South Korea beat Saudi Arabia 2–0 in the League of Legends semifinal. The article also covers selected September 30 compound-team results.

Selected results by competition date

Country-versus-country scores put South Korea first. Kang Yeon-seo and Park Jeong-yun were both Korean entrants in the individual bronze match, but only the winner received bronze.

Mixed compound: a 157–157 tie resolved by shoot-off

Choi Yong-hee and Park Jeong-yun scored 39, 39, 39 and 40 across the four regular ends; India scored 40, 40, 38 and 39. South Korea trailed by one after the third end and drew level in the fourth. In the shoot-off, South Korea’s X and 9 totaled 19; India’s 10 and 10 totaled 20. Here X counts as ten.

The official summary shows 157–157 while a detailed Total presentation shows India 158 and South Korea 157. To avoid mixing regular scores and the deciding marker, this article reports regular total 157–157 and shoot-off 19–20 separately. It does not add them to invent a 176–177 final score.

Team bronze matches: opposite outcomes by one point

Kang Yeon-seo, Park Jeong-yun and Park Ye-rin won women’s team bronze against Chinese Taipei, 229–228. Their ends were 60, 58, 55, 56 versus 59, 58, 56, 55. Both sides had 173 after three ends; the final 56–55 decided the gap.

The men’s team lost 233–234 to Chinese Taipei. South Korea’s ends were 58, 57, 59, 59 versus 59, 59, 57, 59. A three-point deficit after two ends became one after the third, but the fourth was tied. These archery matches took place at Okazaki Chuo Sogo Park’s multipurpose area, not the Aichi Sky Expo shown in the archive photo.

October 1 individual matches

Kim Jong-ho beat CHANG Cheng-Wei of Chinese Taipei 149–145. His ends were 29, 30, 30, 30, 30; the opponent’s were 28, 29, 29, 29, 30. Kang Yeon-seo won the all-Korean bronze match 145–143 against Park Jeong-yun, with ends 30, 29, 29, 28, 29 versus 29, 28, 30, 27, 29. Her team bronze the previous day and individual bronze here are distinct events.

LoL: semifinal victory, not the title yet

South Korea defeated Saudi Arabia 2–0 at Aichi Sky Expo. This is a game-win score, not the in-game kill count. Chinese Taipei defeated Vietnam 2–0 in the other semifinal, making South Korea and Chinese Taipei the finalists. This dated snapshot does not predict or count the final’s medal.
