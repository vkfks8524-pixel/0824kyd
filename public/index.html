Our approach

Show the work.
Keep the uncertainty.

A matching file pair is not a complete backup. A calculator cannot predict your future library. Useful tools say where their answers stop.

See the methods and test fixtures →
  1. Provider facts have sources. Sync and deletion behavior links to the responsible service.
  2. Examples are labeled. Hypothetical libraries are never passed off as reader results.
  3. Tools expose their method. Formulas, known byte fixtures and reproducible tests are published.
  4. Your next action stays specific. Retrieve a sample, compare it, or investigate a missing folder.
