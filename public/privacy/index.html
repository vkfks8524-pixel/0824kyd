PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective: August 22, 2026 · English edition: October 5, 2026.

This policy describes information that may be processed when reading KYD Guides, using its tools or following the external reporting route.

1. Direct input

The site has no membership registration, comments or in-house contact form and does not ask readers for a name, phone number or email to read an article. Reports use GitHub’s public Issues service, where GitHub’s own account and privacy terms apply. Do not publish private or authentication information there.

1.1 Tool inputs

Phone-cost, URL, filename, storage and account-check inputs are processed by JavaScript in the current browser page, not sent to KYD’s server or stored in a database. These tools do not upload files, sign into accounts or query URL reputation. The photo checklist handles selected checkboxes only; it does not read photo or account content or send selections to advertising or analytics services. Inputs and results are not retained by this code after a reload or closing the page.

1.2 Article search

Library search compares the titles and descriptions already in the page. The code does not send search terms to the server, put them in the URL or save them in cookies or browser storage. Topic selection appears after # in the address.

2. Technical request information

Hosting, security and advertising providers may process IP addresses, request times and URLs, browser and device details, referring pages and cookie identifiers to deliver the site, prevent abuse, diagnose faults and provide or measure advertising. Local-only tool input handling does not mean that visiting the website produces no network requests.

3. Cloudflare

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process network-request information for delivery, DNS, security and reliability. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

4. AdSense and cookies

The site includes Google AdSense connection code on content pages. This does not itself mean advertising has been approved or is being displayed. When advertising operates, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology for ads, measurement and fraud prevention, and previous visits may inform personalized advertising. Applicable regional consent requirements must be met before serving ads; this policy is not evidence that a certified consent platform is already configured.

5. Cookie controls

Browser settings can clear or block cookies, which may change personalization and some site behavior. Where a consent interface is provided, use its controls to select or withdraw choices. Browser blocking is not a substitute for a publisher’s applicable consent obligations.

6. External links

Articles link responsible providers, public agencies, photograph sources and GitHub. Their information processing follows their own policies; KYD does not control external account or cookie settings.

7. Retention

KYD does not store the stated tool inputs, but provider technical logs may be retained under their own security and reliability policies. Anything you post publicly on GitHub may remain visible until removed there. Do not submit sensitive information in the first place.

8. Children

The site is not designed to collect personal information from children. Guardians should prevent children from posting private details through public issue reports.

9. Changes and reports

Material changes to advertising, hosting or this description are reflected here and in the relevant update record. Report an error through contact and corrections, without disclosing sensitive details.
