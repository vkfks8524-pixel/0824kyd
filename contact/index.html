CONTACT & CORRECTIONS

Tell us what is wrong or out of date

KYD uses the public GitHub repository’s Issues feature for errors, broken links, changed menus and topic suggestions.

Open a GitHub issue →

This is public. Do not include names, phone numbers, email addresses, account IDs, order references, passwords, verification codes or home addresses. English reports are welcome.

Useful details

  • The KYD article URL.
  • The existing statement and the specific mismatch.
  • Device, operating system, browser or app version.
  • A responsible provider’s official source, if available.

Screenshots

Crop unrelated areas and cover private information with an opaque block rather than weak blur. Check QR codes, barcodes, address bars, tabs, taskbars and notifications. Reopen the final exported image before attaching it. Use the screenshot privacy guide.

How reports are handled

Reports are compared with the article and official sources. We may request more evidence or decline a change that cannot be verified. We do not accept advertising, guaranteed-ranking, paid-link or promotional-placement requests through this route.

Urgent account, device or payment issues

This is not real-time support or an account-recovery service. Contact the relevant official service, financial provider or local authority for urgent harm, account compromise or a lost device. Never send passwords or remote-access codes.
