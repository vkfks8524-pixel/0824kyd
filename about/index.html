ABOUT KYD

Practical decisions, from phone choice to photo storage

KYD Guides is an independent information site connecting phone selection, total purchase cost, photo preservation and device handover. Readers can use their own inputs and check evidence rather than relying on a blanket recommendation. Sports reports and earlier digital-life guides remain topic-based archives.

Who publishes the site?

Articles use KYD Editorial as the site’s publishing name. Generative and automation tools assist drafting, code and editing. This name does not imply a professional testing laboratory, several experts or independently verified qualifications. Official-source explanations, hypothetical examples and genuine direct observations are identified separately.

KYD does not represent manufacturers, broker device sales or provide remote repair services. The purpose is to help readers inspect their own screens and avoid risky assumptions.

What you will find

  • Phone buying decisions, storage planning and purchase-cost calculations.
  • Photo backup, cloud-space cleanup and phone handover checks.
  • Account security, attachments, QR codes and sharing permissions.
  • Browser, file and screenshot guides.
  • Cloudflare Pages domain and deployment guidance.
  • Official-record sports reports with dates, scope and archive-photo attribution.
  • Free browser-based tools that do not upload files or access accounts.

How the information is presented

Articles link the responsible provider or public body, state source-check dates and identify version-dependent steps. Instructions include stop conditions before difficult-to-reverse actions. Reports of changed menus or facts are compared with the original source before correction. See the editorial policy.

English edition and limits

All 32 articles were adapted into English on October 5, 2026. Their original URLs, publication dates and source-review dates are retained. English is not a claim that Korean prices or public-agency reporting contacts apply worldwide. The site does not replace manufacturer support, organizational security policies or individual legal, medical or financial advice. Recheck backups and official guidance before deletion, reset or recovery.
