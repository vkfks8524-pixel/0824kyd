How the calculation works

Net cost = full device principal + initial service × initial months + later service × remaining months + monthly extras × total months + one-off costs and interest − estimated old-phone resale

Including all device principal keeps installments beyond the comparison period visible. This is not a cash-flow forecast. Entering a monthly bill that already includes device installments as service charges would count the device twice.

Cross-check the worked example

The hypothetical 24-month KRW example gives A a total before resale of 2,590,000 and net cost of 2,190,000. B has 3,340,000 before resale and 2,940,000 net: a difference of 750,000 won. Detailed assumptions and manual calculation are published in the article. This is not a current carrier offer or a real customer quote.

Check these conditions separately

  • Do not directly compare rental or device-return plans with an ordinary ownership purchase. Return and buyout obligations differ.
  • Set resale to 0 and compare again if you will not sell the old phone or its value is uncertain.
  • Conditional card benefits, family bundles and early-cancellation charges are not automatic. Enter confirmed terms and check payment and maintenance conditions separately.
  • For three or more rate periods, first calculate each period’s total. Do not guess an average monthly rate.
  • Handle costs common to both quotes, such as remaining old-phone installments, consistently or record them separately.

Verification scope

The public calculation tests examine examples, identical quotes, zero and full initial periods, resale and invalid inputs. They do not verify carrier contracts, actual billing or current promotions.
