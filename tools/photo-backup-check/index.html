    What counts as evidence?

    “I turned backup on” is different from “I retrieved a recent photo and a video from the intended destination.” The second statement identifies an observable result.

    “Independent” means a deletion or account problem in the working library does not automatically remove your only other copy. A second device in the same synchronization setup may not meet that condition.

    Never test by erasing originals. Use a small retrieved sample and keep the source intact.

    What is saved?

    The optional download is a plain-text summary created by your browser. It contains your check results, not account details. KYD does not receive it. Reloading resets the checklist.

    How the result is calculated

    Each unchecked item stays on the next-action list. There is no weighted score or “safe to delete” state, even with all six marked. The test method covers all 64 checkbox combinations.
